About CoverageCheckin

Why we exist.

CoverageCheckin helps Utah homeowners stay ahead of renewals and non-renewals and connects them to licensed independent agents. We are a referral service, not an insurer or agency.

Disclosure

CoverageCheckin is not an insurance agency or insurance company. We do not sell, quote, or recommend insurance policies, and nothing on this site is insurance advice. We may refer you to a licensed Utah insurance agent. Any quotes, coverage advice, or policy decisions come from that agent and from you.
