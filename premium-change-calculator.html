Utah's comparison tables

The Utah Insurance Department publishes annual homeowners insurance comparison tables showing estimated premiums from large Utah insurers for sample homes in several ZIP codes. Your own premium depends on your home, coverage, and insurer, so it may differ from those examples.

Questions about a renewal offer are best taken to a licensed agent or to your insurer. You can also set a renewal check reminder.

General information only, not insurance advice. Ask a licensed agent about your situation.
