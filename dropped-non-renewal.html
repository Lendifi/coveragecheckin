Got a non-renewal notice?

Don't wait until the last week. A licensed agent can look at options with you.

What to do first

A short checklist

  1. Find the end date

    Note the date your current coverage ends. It's on the notice.

  2. Check your mortgage

    If you have a mortgage, your lender may require continuous coverage. Check your loan documents.

  3. Know the Utah rules

    For notice periods and your rights, see the Utah Insurance Department at insurance.utah.gov and our guide to what happens after a notice.

  4. Talk with a licensed agent

    An agent can go over your options. We can refer you to a licensed Utah agent if you'd like.
