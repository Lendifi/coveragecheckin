Editorial policy

How we write, source, and correct our pages.

What we publish

General information for Utah homeowners about renewals, non-renewals, and how to find a licensed agent. We do not publish insurance advice, recommend carriers, or compare specific policies.

Sourcing

  • Every statistic links to a public source, such as the Utah Insurance Department, Utah Code, or a government report. If we cannot source a number, we leave it out.
  • Regulatory statements are checked against the primary source on the date shown on the page.
  • We do not publish made-up stories, testimonials, or reviews.

Money and independence

No agency or carrier can pay to change what we write. See How we make money.

Corrections

Spot an error? Email hello@coveragecheckin.com. We review correction requests and note material changes on the page with a date.

General information only, not insurance advice. Ask a licensed agent about your situation.

CoverageCheckin is not an insurance agency or insurance company. We do not sell, quote, or recommend insurance policies, and nothing on this site is insurance advice. We may refer you to a licensed Utah insurance agent. Any quotes, coverage advice, or policy decisions come from that agent and from you.
